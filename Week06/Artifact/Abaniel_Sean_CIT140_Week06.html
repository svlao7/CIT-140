System: Inspection Report Routing System

Inside the boundary

The Inspection Report Routing System does four things: 1. Receive Report accepts the Field Team's inspection report and writes a submitted report to D1 Report Register; 2. Check Required Fields , sends a validation note to the Field Team and passes accepted reports. A rejected report does not continue; 3. Resolve Destination reads routing entries from D2 Routing Table; 4 Send Work Item sends a work item to the resolved department and writes a delivery record to D1. 

Outside the boundary

External teams are the Field Team, Electrical, Drainage and Structures departments. Outside the boundary are flight control, physical inspections and repair work. A service administrator maintains the Routing Table.

Supported finding

CJ-603 is a BRIDGE report (asset_type in W06-S02, row CJ-603). The destination policy says a BRIDGE report belongs to Structures (W06-S03), but the delivery log shows CJ-603 was ACCEPTED and received by Electrical (W06-S04). The expected department (Structures) and the observed department (Electrical) differ, so acceptance by the field check did not mean correct routing.
